      Prices stream live from Finnhub while the US market is open (9:30 AM–4:00 PM Eastern) and fall back to a quote refresh every 30 seconds. Outside market hours you trade at the last price. Short selling: the sale proceeds go into your cash and 50% of the short's value is held as margin on top, so each short uses half its value in buying power.
